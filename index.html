Say what

A weekend meditation retreat at Blue Cliff Monastery in Pine Bush, NY — a monastery in the Plum Village tradition of Thich Nhat Hanh. We drive up together on Friday Oct 30, spend Saturday (Halloween) in noble (spooky) silence, and head back to the city Sunday with a stop at NY Makgeolli Brewery & Pub for makgeolli 𓏊 and eel 𓆓 on the way home.

Logistics

  • Cost: adult dorm is $80–$120 per night (sliding scale), so about $160–$240 per person for the two nights. Meals included.
  • Lodging: shared dorms. Simple, clean, monastic. Bring a sense of humor.
  • What to expect: vegan meals, early mornings, sitting and walking meditation, and one full day of noble silence. Beginners very welcome — that's us too.
  • What to bring: modest, comfortable clothes; layers (it's the Catskills-adjacent countryside in late October); toiletries; a water bottle; maybe a journal.
